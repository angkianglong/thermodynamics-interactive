Quick Presets:
Teaching model: 1 kg of air modeled as an ideal gas with constant specific heats, initially at 300 K and 1 atm. Values used: cv = 0.718 kJ/(kg·K), cp = 1.005 kJ/(kg·K), and R = 0.287 kJ/(kg·K), satisfying cp − cv = R. For the same heat input, ΔT = Q/(m c), so constant-volume heating produces a larger temperature rise because cv < cp. The 18 blue dots are representative air molecules: their number remains fixed. As Q increases, their visual speed increases progressively; at the same Q, the hotter Cv gas moves slightly faster than the Cp gas.

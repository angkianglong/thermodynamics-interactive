Quick Presets:
Teaching model: 1 mol ideal monatomic gas, initially at 300 K and 1 atm. For the same heat input, ΔT = Q/(nC), so the constant-volume gas warms more because Cv < Cp. At constant pressure, part of the heat input becomes boundary work.
